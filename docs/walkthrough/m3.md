# M3 walkthrough: collaboration

What to try, and what to look for, after upgrading the office to M3 (SPEC §10
M3). It also covers the fixes from the M2.5 walkthrough. Allow about 45
minutes, and a colleague (or a second browser in a private window) for the
multiplayer parts. Screenshots for each feature are in `docs/screenshots/<issue>/`.

Tick each box as you go; anything that looks wrong is worth an issue, with a
screenshot and what you did.

## 1. Upgrade

1. Take a backup first: `cd deploy && docker compose exec backup scripts/backup.sh`.
2. On the server: `scripts/setup.sh --upgrade`. It runs two database migrations:
   0018 renames floors to operations and robots to henchmen (#226), and 0019
   adds meetings (#50). Both rename or add in place; no rows are copied out.
3. For voice and the lounge TV, also run `scripts/setup.sh --media` and open
   the firewall ports in [`docs/deploy/media.md`](../deploy/media.md)
   (UDP 7882, TCP 7881). Without it the office works; voice is just hidden.

Look for:

- [ ] Every service healthy in `docker compose ps` (and `livekit` with `--media`).
- [ ] Your operations, their members and every seated henchman are where they were.
- [ ] Nothing in the UI says *floor* or *robot* any more.

## 2. Fixes from the M2.5 walkthrough

- [ ] **Running** (#223): hold **Shift** while walking (WASD, a click path, first person), or **double-click** a spot. A colleague sees you run.
- [ ] **Existing GitHub App** (#224): Settings → Office → **Use an existing GitHub App…** with your original app (App ID, `.pem`, webhook secret). It shows the webhook URL to set and lists any missing permissions or events.
- [ ] **Settings** (#225): a wide dialog with tabs (You, Office, Henchmen, Notifications, Display and sound). Henchmen: rules as cards with skin thumbnails, top rule wins, up/down to reorder.
- [ ] **Issue board** (#237): an issue sits in **In progress** only while a henchman is starting, working or waiting on you for it. Assignees and labels show on the card but no longer move it; a queued task shows a **Queued** chip.

## 3. Whiteboard (#45)

- [ ] Each operation room and the lobby (north wall) has a whiteboard. Click it or press **E** to open the editor.
- [ ] Two people draw at once and see each other's strokes and named cursors.
- [ ] After closing, the wall shows a snapshot of the board.
- [ ] Someone with view access only can look but not draw.

## 4. Wall pictures (#46)

- [ ] Rooms panel → **Hang a picture…** in an operation room: pick a PNG, JPG or WebP (up to 10 MB).
- [ ] The ghost is green on free wall and red, with a reason, over boards, the whiteboard, doors and other pictures. **Enter** or a click hangs it.
- [ ] A colleague in the room sees it appear without reloading.
- [ ] Click your picture: drag to move, corner handles to resize, **Del** to remove.

## 5. Jukebox (#47)

- [ ] In the lobby, walk to the jukebox and press **E**. Four bundled tracks are in the library; upload your own audio or add a YouTube link.
- [ ] Two browsers hear the same track at the same point.
- [ ] It is loud in the lobby, fainter in the corridors and almost silent deep in a room.
- [ ] Settings → Display and sound: the volume slider works, and **Mute the jukebox** silences it for you only.

## 6. Chat, emotes, sitting, who's where (#49)

- [ ] A chat line floats as a bubble over the speaker's head for a few seconds, on both browsers.
- [ ] Hold **G** for the emote wheel (wave, thumbs up, clap, dance, point, facepalm), or press 1–6. The other browser sees it.
- [ ] Press **E** at the lobby sofa, an armchair, the break-room couch or a war-room chair to sit; **E** again to stand. Two people cannot take one seat; desk seats stay for henchmen.
- [ ] The **Who's where** panel lists everyone's room and what they are doing. Clicking a name walks you there, stopping at the door of a room you cannot enter.

## 7. Voice and the lounge TV (#48, needs `--media`)

- [ ] The voice strip in the HUD has mic and share buttons; **M** toggles the mic (push-to-talk in Settings).
- [ ] A colleague's voice gets quieter as you walk apart and is gone from about 17 m; their mouth moves while they talk; a **Muted** badge shows when they mute.
- [ ] Voices in an operation room are only heard by people in that room.
- [ ] **Share** puts your screen on the lobby's lounge TV. Sitting on the lobby sofa opens it full screen; standing up closes it. Only one person shares at a time.
- [ ] Owner to-do: try it from two different networks (home and mobile), since the gate only tested it locally.

## 8. Meeting room (#50, needs a runner)

- [ ] Rooms panel → **Meeting room…** in an operation room: pick 2–5 henchmen (any provider), a pattern (debate, lead and team, map-reduce, red/blue, review panel), rounds and a token budget, and an output (draft PR, PR review, or notes only).
- [ ] The hologram over the meeting table shows the pattern, the round, who has the floor and the budget left; the door sign says **Meeting in session**.
- [ ] Only you (the starter) can pause, resume or stop it; colleagues with access watch.
- [ ] When it ends you get the draft PR, the review on the PR, or the notes, and the henchmen go home.
- [ ] Owner to-do: run one real meeting on a small task with real Claude Code and Codex; the gate used a fake `claude`.

## 9. Known gaps (filed)

- #232: a few e2e checks time out on a heavily loaded machine; CI is green.
- #248: the gong e2e step is still occasionally flaky and, because the suite is serial, hides the steps after it; a fix is in progress.
- Rooms with a single desk have no lounge seats for humans yet; walk-to-teammate does not follow someone who keeps moving; the jukebox has no "delete track"; pictures hang only in operation rooms.
