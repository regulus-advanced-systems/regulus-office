The HUD restyle (#189) is an original design in the 1960s lair control-panel genre (brass frames, rivets, stencil nameplates, lamps, hazard stripes are generic period motifs drawn in CSS); no logo, UI art, layout or font comes from any commercial game.

The lair kit follows SPEC D23: it matches the 1960s spy-villain-lair genre in palette, proportions, materials, lighting and silhouettes, and does not take, trace or rebuild any model, texture, logo, UI or level layout from a commercial game.
