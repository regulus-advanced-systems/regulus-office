## Lair art kit (#183)

The lair kit (rock-cut walls, floors, corridors, blast doors, lights, pipes, consoles, lair furniture, plants and build-phase props) adds no files to this package: every piece is procedural geometry authored in this repository (`apps/web/src/scene/lair/geometry/`), original work under the project's MIT licence. Following SPEC D23, it matches the 1960s spy-villain-lair genre in palette, proportions, materials, lighting and silhouettes. It does not take, trace or rebuild any model, texture, logo, UI or level layout from a commercial game.
